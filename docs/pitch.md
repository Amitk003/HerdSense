# HerdSense Pitch

## The problem

Current drought early-warning systems rely on satellite data. The problem is satellites take 10 to 14 days to report a problem. By then, livestock have already lost condition. Herders have lost money. Communities are already in crisis.

## What we do

HerdSense uses the animal itself as the sensor. Animals show stress days before satellites see anything. They bunch together. They walk differently. Their heads droop. They make distress sounds. HerdSense measures these signals using computer vision running on your phone and gives a clear stress score.

## How we are different

**Satellite-based systems**: Update every 10-14 days. See large areas only. Detect problems after damage is done.

**HerdSense**: Updates in real time. Works at the herd level. Detects problems before they are visible from space. Runs on the phone in your pocket. Works without internet.

## The technology

All machine learning runs in the browser. No data leaves your phone unless you choose to share an anonymous score with nearby users. Sharing goes directly from phone to phone over WebRTC - no server involved. The app works offline. No cell signal needed after initial load.

## What it costs

Nothing. The app is free. No subscription. No hardware to buy. No installation fees. Just open the web app and point your phone at your herd.

## The vision

We want to give every herder the same information that satellite systems provide, but faster, cheaper, and more locally relevant. HerdSense does not replace the herder's knowledge. It amplifies it.
